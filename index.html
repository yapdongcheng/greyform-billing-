    Item codes and rates used

    From past ERP invoices and the trip sheet's rate columns. Standby is time on site over 4 hours, rounded to the nearest half hour. Overtime applies when the truck leaves after 21:00. GST 9%. Due date is 45 days after the last trip.

    How to use it
    1. Click Choose billing file (or drag the Excel file onto the page). The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
    2. Pick a project on the left. Check its total against the trip sheet.
    3. Click Copy lines for ERP, then click the first empty line in the ERP invoice and paste.
    4. Download Excel with tables gives back your billing file with nothing removed, and the invoice table added below the trips on each project tab.
